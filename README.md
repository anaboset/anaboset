## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 💼 At Future Interns — Real-World ML Systems

| Project | Description | Stack | Skills |
|---------|-------------|-------|--------|
| **📝 Resume Intelligence System** | Automated resume screening & ranking engine based on job role requirements | `Python` `spaCy` `NLTK` `Scikit-learn` | Text analysis, feature extraction, resume scoring, ranking models |
| **🎫 Smart Support Triage** | NLP-powered customer support ticket classification with auto-priority assignment | `Python` `NLTK` `spaCy` `Scikit-learn` | Text preprocessing, NLP classification, priority logic, support analytics |
| **📈 Demand Forecasting Engine** | Time-series model to forecast future sales/demand using historical business data | `Python` `Matplotlib` `Power BI` `Tableau` | Time-series analysis, forecasting, business interpretation |

### 🫀 Flagship Project — CardioSentinel

> **AI-Powered Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) Management System**

A production-oriented health-tech AI system supporting cardiovascular disease management with modern LLM architectures and clinical reasoning.

- 🧠 **Multi-agent AI architecture** for clinical workflows
- 📚 **RAG-based guideline retrieval** for evidence-grounded responses
- ⚕️ **Clinical decision support** aligned with real healthcare practices
- 🛡️ **Patient safety-first** design with explainability & ethical AI guardrails
- 🧩 **Built for scale** — from prototype to real-world deployment
